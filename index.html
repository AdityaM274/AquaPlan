Why this is more than cheapest-route-first

The solver first minimizes priority-weighted unmet demand, then transport cost among equally good allocations. A min-cost flow network includes an explicit unmet-demand route, so impossible requests remain visible. It does not promise fairness or a deployable emergency plan.
